What nobal.news is

“An empowering way to follow the world”

nobal.news turns the traditional news homepage into a spatial, context-first tool that returns agency to users pursuing understanding of specific topics and places. nobal.news allows users to...

  • 1. Access customized media streams
  • 2. Understand diverse perspectives
  • 3. Build community around healthy media consumption and discourse

How nobal works

“Global stories in a few seconds.”

The globe serves as the entry point - a lens through which individuals can intuitively discover more. Apply your topic filter, scan the globe, and explore, store & share perspectives.

  • 1. Use nobal algorithm-builder to select specific topic set(s) related to your interests
  • 2. Analyze global perspectives via a traditional media formats or via a media-tagged globe
  • 3. Save media of interest, collect your unique thoughts and share both with your peers

Product system

“Tools built to empower”

Nobal is designed for media consumption & source analysis whilst also providing a long term home for information of interest and your perspectives. Our backbone is:

  • Our novel algorithm selection system
  • Our innovative media consumptions interfaces
  • Our visual-spatial media storage system
  • Our provision of source background data
